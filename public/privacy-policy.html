Privacy Policy

LingoSphere AI Language Tutor • Effective Date: October 2026
Summary of Our Commitment:

LingoSphere is committed to protecting your privacy. We process speech and voice audio solely for live language tutoring and AI conversation practice. We never sell your personal data or speech recordings to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google or Email, we store your User ID, Display Name, and Email address to manage your learning progress.
  • Audio & Speech Data: Real-time microphone audio is converted to text to enable two-way conversation with the AI Tutor. Audio streams are processed ephemerally during active calls and are not stored permanently.
  • Learning Progress & Activity: Lessons completed, vocabulary flashcards (Leitner box progression), daily streaks, speaking turns, and quiz scores.
  • Subscription Data: Purchase tokens and subscription tier (Free or Premium) verified via Google Play Billing.

2. How We Use Information

  • To deliver real-time AI voice calling and customized language instruction in Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and other target languages.
  • To personalize learning curriculum based on your selected proficiency level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
  • To generate post-call learning reports including vocabulary learned and grammar corrections.
  • To enforce daily speaking call limits (5 minutes daily for free users, 50 minutes for premium users).

3. Audio & Voice Data Processing

Voice audio is captured using standard Android Audio APIs and processed with Google Cloud / Gemini AI APIs solely for the purpose of conversational transcription, language evaluation, and real-time response generation. Audio data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your user profile, learning statistics, and flashcards for as long as your account is active. You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open LingoSphere → Profile → "Delete Account & All Data".
  • Web Deletion Request: Visit our public deletion request page: Data Deletion Request Portal.

5. Security of Your Information

We protect user data using Google Firebase Firestore zero-trust security rules, end-to-end HTTPS encryption, and tokenized Google Identity Authentication.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at support@lingosphere.app.
