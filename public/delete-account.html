Account & Data Deletion Request

Google Play Data Safety & GDPR / CCPA Compliance

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global data privacy regulations, LingoSphere users have the permanent right to delete their account and request deletion of all associated personal and learning data.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can instantly and permanently wipe your account and all data directly within the application without waiting:

  1. Open the LingoSphere app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile icon on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Danger Zone.
  4. Tap Delete Account & All Data and confirm.
  5. Your user profile, flashcards, lesson progress, call recordings, transcripts, and credentials are permanently purged immediately from Google Firebase Firestore and Auth.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, submit your registered account details below. Our automated deletion pipeline will process your request within 48 hours and send a confirmation to your email.

✓ Your deletion request has been registered successfully. All profile data, flashcards, speech sessions, and login credentials associated with this email will be permanently wiped.

What Data Is Deleted?

  • User Account Profile (User ID, Display Name, Target Language, Streaks, XP points).
  • Cloud Private Subcollection (Email addresses and verification records).
  • All Lesson Progress records (`lesson_progress`).
  • All Leitner Spaced-Repetition Flashcards (`flashcards`).
  • All Speaking Session Transcripts & Voice Call Learning Reports (`speaking_sessions`).
  • Firebase Authentication User Credentials.

Data Retention Exceptions

No personal data is retained following account deletion. Anonymized, non-personally identifiable billing transaction records may be retained only where strictly required by applicable tax or financial reporting laws.
